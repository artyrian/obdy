    About

    Built and tested on a Ford Mustang 2.3 EcoBoost with a UniCarScan UCSI-2100 (BLE ELM327). Standard OBD-II (live PIDs, DTCs, freeze frame) should work on most 2008+ cars with CAN.

    Ford-tuned parts may not fit other cars: Mode 06 misfire MIDs, normal ranges (ref), guided-test thresholds and the P008A notes. Not a replacement for a professional scan tool.

    Manufacturer values come from OBDb (CC BY-SA 4.0); VINs are decoded with the NHTSA vPIC service.
